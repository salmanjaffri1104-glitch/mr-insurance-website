Endowment Plan

Savings + Protection

← All Conventional Plans

This plan gives you both savings and protection — you receive the amount at the end of the term, and if anything happens in between, your family still gets complete protection.

What this plan offers

  • At maturity: if you survive the term, you receive the Sum Assured + accrued Bonus
  • On death (God forbid): if death occurs during the term, the full Sum Assured + Bonus is paid to your heirs
  • This is a with-profit plan — you share in State Life’s annual bonus
  • Once at least 2 years of premiums are paid, the policy acquires a Surrender Value
  • The policy can be cancelled within 14 days of receiving it (free look period)

Eligibility and term

  • Entry age: minimum 10 years, maximum 65 years
  • Maximum age at maturity: 75 years
  • Premiums: yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly — payable until the end of the term or death, whichever comes first
  • Extra cover (riders): additional cover such as Accidental Death Benefit can be added — on accidental death, an extra amount equal to the basic Sum Assured is paid

How much will the premium be?

The premium depends on your age, Sum Assured and term. A real example: age 30, term 20 years, Sum Assured Rs 500,000 — yearly premium Rs 24,060 (monthly Rs 2,165). You can also add the Family Income Benefit (FIB) and Accidental Death Benefit (ADB) riders — the calculator below includes them. Use the calculator to work out your own premium, or share your age and required amount on WhatsApp to get your exact figure.

Child Education & Marriage Plan

Preparing for Your Child’s Future

← All Conventional Plans

The Child Education & Marriage Plan is made for one purpose: your child’s future. It provides a lump sum when your child reaches the age where money matters most — 18, 21 or 25 years — the time of higher education, marriage, or setting up a business.

How it works

  • You choose the target age: 18, 21 or 25 years
  • At the end of the term, the child receives the full Sum Assured + accrued Bonuses
  • The amount can be taken as a lump sum, or in five equal annual instalments

Who can take this plan

  • Child’s age at entry: 1 to 15 years
  • Policyholder’s age at entry: 20 to 60 years
  • Plan term: 10 to 24 years (set so the amount is payable at age 18, 21 or 25)
  • Parents can take this plan — and so can grandparents, uncles, aunts, or anyone who pays for the child’s maintenance

If the child passes away during the term (God forbid)

The policyholder can choose:

  • Continue the same plan for another child, or
  • Take a refund of all premiums paid or the policy’s cash value — whichever is higher — or
  • Continue the policy without naming another child

Good to know

  • Premium is level — the same every year — payable till maturity
  • The Family Income Benefit (FIB) Rider can be attached separately (this plan has no built-in income benefit)
  • ADB (Accidental Death Benefit) rider is also available
  • No medical evidence is required on the child’s life

A real example: Policyholder age 30, child age 5, amount payable at age 25, Sum Assured Rs 500,000 → annual premium Rs 24,060 (monthly instalment Rs 2,165).

Jeevan Sathi Plan

One Policy — Protection for Both Lives

← All Conventional Plans

Jeevan Sathi is a joint-life plan for a husband and wife. One policy covers both lives, with one Sum Assured — so the protection continues even if one of you is no longer there.

How it works

  • Premiums are payable until the end of the term, or the first death, whichever comes first.
  • On the first death, the Sum Assured is paid to the surviving spouse — and all further premiums are waived. The surviving spouse’s cover continues, and the policy keeps sharing in bonuses.
  • On the second death, the Sum Assured is paid again, together with the bonuses — a double payment of protection from a single plan.
  • If the surviving spouse lives to the end of the term, the Sum Assured and bonuses are paid at maturity — even though the Sum Assured was already paid once.
  • If both of you survive the term, the Sum Assured and bonuses are paid at maturity.
  • Both lives are medically examined, and two proposal forms are completed — one for each life.

Terms available: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 or 35 years

How the premium is calculated

The premium is based on an Equivalent Age. If both of you are the same age, that age is used. If your ages differ, a small deduction (from the State Life Uniform Seniority Table) is made from the elder spouse’s age — so an age gap does not push the premium up unfairly. Try it yourself in the calculator below.

Extra protection

  • ADB (Accidental Death Benefit) can be added for both lives. Note: special terms and conditions (T&C) apply to ADB on the woman’s life — please ask for details.

A real example (from the official rate book): Husband aged 30, wife aged 30, 20-year term, Sum Assured Rs 500,000 → Annual Premium Rs 28,010 — just Rs 2,521 per month to protect both lives with one policy.

Note: The premium shown by the calculator is an estimate. The final premium is decided by State Life’s policy and underwriting.

Golden Endowment Plan

Pay for 7 Years — Covered for 20 Years

← All Conventional Plans

Golden Endowment is a special endowment plan with one big advantage: you pay premiums for only 7 years, while your policy runs for a full 20 years. After 7 years, you stop paying — and your protection and bonuses keep growing until maturity.

Salient features

  • Policy term: 20 years
  • Premium term: only 7 years — no premiums after the 7th year
  • Entry age: 20 to 55 years
  • Premium mode: yearly only
  • Death benefit: Sum Assured + accrued bonuses
  • Maturity benefit: Sum Assured + bonuses at the end of 20 years
  • Bonuses are declared at the same rates as the Endowment Assurance plan (20-year term)

Extra protection

  • ADB (Accidental Death Benefit) can be added up to age 40.

A real example (from the official rate book): Age 30, Sum Assured Rs 500,000 → Annual Premium Rs 53,275 — payable for 7 years only, while the policy matures at 20 years.

Whole Life Plan

Protection for the Whole of Life

← All Conventional Plans

Whole Life Assurance is a unique combination of protection and savings that can be purchased at a very economical premium. There is no fixed policy term to choose — your cover runs for the whole of your life, and premiums are payable until death or until the policy anniversary nearest age 85, when the policy matures.

Salient features

  • Cover: for the whole of life — the policy matures at the policy anniversary nearest age 85
  • Entry age: 10 to 65 years (the ages priced in the official rate table)
  • Premium modes: yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly
  • Death benefit: Sum Assured + bonuses (if any)
  • Maturity benefit: Sum Assured + bonuses at the policy anniversary nearest age 85
  • With profits: the rate book notes that bonus rates under this plan are usually much higher than those of the other plans
  • After two years’ premiums have been paid, the policy acquires surrender and non-forfeiture values, and a policy loan of up to 80% of the surrender value can be taken

Extra protection

  • ADB (Accidental Death Benefit) can be added — standard rate Rs 1.25 per Rs 1,000 of Sum Assured.

A real example (calculated from the official rate book): Age 30, Sum Assured Rs 500,000 → Annual Premium Rs 10,105 — or just Rs 909 per month.

Note: The premium shown by the calculator is an estimate. The final premium is decided by State Life’s policy and underwriting.

Smart Protection · Flexible Investment · Secure Future

← All Conventional Plans

Titanium is a single premium universal life insurance plan. You invest one time only — there are no yearly or monthly premiums — and your money combines life protection with investment growth over a coverage term of 2 to 15 years.

Salient features

Personal Pension Scheme

Pay Once — Pension Guaranteed for 25 Years, Then for Life

← All Conventional Plans

The Personal Pension Scheme is a single premium pension plan: you pay one premium, once — there are no yearly or monthly premiums after that — and in return you receive a guaranteed pension. It is designed for lifetime security, especially for self-employed people and those whose organisations have no pension scheme.

Salient features

  • Single Premium: pay once only — minimum Rs 50,000, no maximum
  • Entry age: 18 years and above — no upper age limit
  • No medical evidence required — the plan is issued regardless of health
  • Pension guaranteed for 25 years — and if you outlive the 25 years, the pension continues for life
  • First pension payment at the beginning of the 8th policy year; the pension amount depends on your entry age and sex (the rate book’s chart)
  • On death after the pension starts (before 25 years of pension are paid), your nominee(s) keep receiving the pension for the balance of the 25 years
  • Full encashment option after 6 years: instead of the pension, take a lump sum equal to double the single premium
  • Part encashment (25% / 50% / 75%) with a reduced pension is also allowed, as long as the remaining pension is at least Rs 2,500 per year
  • Monthly pension option available on request when the yearly pension exceeds Rs 36,000

Note: A company fee is charged in addition to the single premium, at the fee rate applicable in the month the policy is issued (the fee is revised every month). Please contact us for the current month’s fee.

A real example (from the official rate book): Male, age 35, Single Premium Rs 100,000 → yearly pension Rs 9,852, starting from the 8th policy year — guaranteed for 25 years, and for life if he lives beyond them. Use the calculator below to work out your own pension.

Term Insurance — Nigehban Plan

Pure Protection — Big Cover at the Lowest Premium

← All Conventional Plans

The Term Insurance plan — the Nigehban Plan — is a pure protection plan. It carries no savings or maturity component, which is why it gives the largest life cover for the lowest premium: if death occurs during the chosen term while the policy is in force, the full sum assured is paid to your family.

Salient features

  • Entry age: 20 to 60 years (age nearest birthday)
  • Term: 5 to 10 years — you choose
  • Two ways to pay: yearly premiums (Table 24 — yearly mode only, there is no monthly mode in this plan), or a single premium paid once for the whole term (Table 25)
  • Pure death cover: sum assured is payable on death during the term; there is no maturity benefit, no surrender value and no loan value, and the plan is without profits
  • Accidental Death Benefit (ADB) rider may be attached at Rs 1.25 per Rs 1,000 of sum assured (on the single premium plan, the rider premium for the whole term is charged once, at the start)
  • The normal Non-Medical Scheme and underwriting requirements apply

A real example (from the official rate book): Age 30, term 10 years, Sum Assured Rs 500,000 → yearly premium only Rs 2,550 (Table 24), or a single premium of Rs 22,440 once for the full 10 years (Table 25).   • آپ کا خاندان آپ کی آمدنی پر انحصار کرتا ہے: خدا نخواستہ اگر آپ کو کچھ ہو جائے تو سم ایشورڈ (Sum Assured) آپ کے گھر کے اخراجات، بچوں کی تعلیم اور ان کی شادیوں کا سہارا بنتا ہے۔
  • چھوٹی قسط، بڑے نقصان سے حفاظت: ایک معمولی باقاعدہ پریمیم آپ کے خاندان کو اس اچانک مالی دھچکے سے بچاتا ہے جس کا بوجھ عام بچت اکیلی نہیں اٹھا سکتی۔
  • جتنی جلدی شروع کریں، اتنا ہی کم خرچ: کم عمر میں پریمیم بھی کم ہوتا ہے، اس لیے پالیسی جلد لینے کا مطلب ہے — وہی تحفظ، کم قیمت میں۔
  • ذہنی سکون: آپ اپنے کام اور خاندان پر پوری توجہ دے سکتے ہیں، یہ جانتے ہوئے کہ حالات جیسے بھی ہوں، ان کا مستقبل محفوظ ہے۔
  • قرض اور ذمہ داریوں کا تحفظ: اگر گھر کا قرض یا کاروبار کی کوئی ذمہ داری ہو تو حادثے کی صورت میں اس کا بوجھ آپ کے خاندان پر نہیں پڑتا — انشورنس کے کور سے قرض ادا کیا جا سکتا ہے اور گھر و کاروبار محفوظ رہتا ہے۔
  • بڑی رقم کا فوری بندوبست مشکل ہوتا ہے: اچانک لاکھوں روپے جوڑنا کسی کے لیے بھی آسان نہیں ہوتا — لیکن چھوٹی باقاعدہ قسط سے بڑا کور پہلے ہی دن سے تیار رہتا ہے، جب بھی ضرورت پڑے۔
  • نوکری اور کاروبار کی غیر یقینی صورتِ حال: ذاتی انشورنس ہر حال میں آپ کے ساتھ رہتی ہے — ملازمت بدلنے یا کاروبار کے اتار چڑھاؤ سے آپ کا کور ختم نہیں ہوتا۔
  • خاندان کا معیارِ زندگی برقرار رہتا ہے: بچوں کی تعلیم اور گھر کا نظام حادثے کے بعد بھی نہیں رکتا — وہی اسکول، وہی طرزِ زندگی، بغیر کسی سمجھوتے کے۔
  • جلد فیصلے کا فائدہ: کم عمر اور اچھی صحت میں پالیسی کی منظوری بھی آسان ہوتی ہے اور پریمیم بھی کم — جتنی تاخیر ہوگی، اتنا ہی یہ فائدہ کم ہوتا جائے گا۔

انشورنس کے فائدے کیا ہیں؟

  • خاندان کی حفاظت: مدت کے دوران وفات کی صورت میں (خدا نخواستہ) مکمل سم ایشورڈ آپ کے خاندان کو دیا جاتا ہے — پالیسی کی شرائط کے مطابق۔
  • حفاظت کے ساتھ بچت: اینڈومنٹ جیسے بچت والے پلانز میں لائف کور کے ساتھ باقاعدہ بچت بھی ہوتی ہے — میچورٹی پر رقم بونس کے ساتھ ملتی ہے، پالیسی کی شرائط کے مطابق۔
  • رائیڈرز سے اضافی تحفظ: ADB (حادثاتی وفات کا فائدہ) رائیڈر حادثاتی موت پر اضافی کور دیتا ہے، اور FIB (فیملی انکم بینیفٹ) رائیڈر آپ کے خاندان کو باقاعدہ آمدنی فراہم کر سکتا ہے۔
  • قرض (لون) کی سہولت: بچت والے پلانز میں، پالیسی کی شرائط کے مطابق، سرنڈر ویلیو بن جانے کے بعد پالیسی پر قرض لیا جا سکتا ہے۔
  • مقاصد کی منصوبہ بندی: بچوں کی تعلیم و شادی ہو یا ریٹائرمنٹ کی پنشن — ہدف کے مطابق چنا گیا پلان خواہش کو ایک مکمل منصوبے میں بدل دیتا ہے۔
  • یہ کور آپ کا اپنا ہے: یہ کسی نوکری سے بندھا ہوا نہیں — نوکری بدلے یا اپنا کاروبار شروع کریں، یہ تحفظ آپ کے ساتھ رہتا ہے۔

انشورنس سے بچت کیسے ہوتی ہے؟

بچت ایک عادت ہے، اور پریمیم اس عادت کو نظم و ضبط دیتا ہے۔ ایک مقررہ پریمیم — ماہانہ، سہ ماہی یا سالانہ — وہ عہد ہے جو آپ نبھاتے ہیں، اور چھوٹی چھوٹی باقاعدہ رقوم سالوں میں ایک بڑی رقم بن جاتی ہیں۔ گھر میں یا اکاؤنٹ میں رکھی ہوئی بچت اکثر روزمرہ ضروریات میں خرچ ہو جاتی ہے؛ پریمیم کے طور پر دی گئی بچت محفوظ رہتی ہے — اور جب تک وہ بڑھتی ہے، آپ کا خاندان پہلے ہی دن سے محفوظ ہوتا ہے۔

اس طرح سوچیں: ماہانہ 5,000 روپے بچانا آسان ہے — اور خرچ کر دینا اس سے بھی آسان۔ یہی رقم پریمیم کے طور پر دی جائے تو یہی عادت ایک بڑا سم ایشورڈ بناتی ہے جو پہلے دن سے آپ کے خاندان کی حفاظت کرتا ہے — اور بچت والے پلانز میں میچورٹی کی رقم بھی ملتی ہے، پالیسی کی شرائط کے مطابق۔

سمجھ نہیں آ رہا کہاں سے شروع کریں؟ ہر پلان کے پیج پر کیلکولیٹر موجود ہے جو دونوں طرف چلتا ہے: مطلوبہ کور منتخب کریں اور پریمیم دیکھیں — یا اپنی ماہانہ بچت درج کریں اور دیکھیں کہ اس سے کتنا کور مل سکتا ہے۔ پلانز اور کیلکولیٹرز دیکھیں

نوٹ: ہر پلان مختلف ہوتا ہے۔ ٹرم انشورنس (نگہبان پلان) صرف حفاظت ہے — اس میں کوئی میچورٹی رقم نہیں ملتی — جبکہ بچت والے پلانز حفاظت اور بچت دونوں دیتے ہیں۔ حتمی اعداد و شمار اور فوائد اسٹیٹ لائف کی پالیسی شرائط اور انڈر رائٹنگ کے مطابق ہوتے ہیں — کسی بھی منافع کی پیشگی ضمانت نہیں دی جاتی۔

Takaful Endowment Plan

Shariah-Compliant Protection with Bonuses and Savings

← All Takaful Plans

Takaful Endowment is a bonus-based, Shariah-compliant plan that combines life protection with savings. Your contributions are shared between the Participant Takaful Endowment Fund (PTEF), which provides your Sum Cover and declared bonuses, and the Participant Investment Endowment Fund (PIEF), which builds your investment value — family protection and disciplined savings in one membership.

Salient features

  • Entry age: 18 to 65 years
  • Contribution paying term: 10 to 57 years — offered where entry age + term does not exceed 75 (maximum maturity age 75)
  • Contributions: calculated from the rate book’s contribution table for your age and term, applied per Rs 1,000 of Sum Cover, and shown here on an annual basis
  • Minimum contribution: Annual Rs 15,000 · Semi-Annual Rs 10,000 · Quarterly Rs 5,000 · Monthly Rs 2,500
  • Death benefit: Sum Cover plus declared bonuses from the PTEF, and in addition the carrying value of the PIEF is payable to the nominee
  • Maturity benefit: Sum Cover and declared bonuses from the PTEF, in addition to the carrying value of the PIEF
  • Supplementary Takaful benefits (riders): Accidental Death Benefit (ADB), Accidental Death and Indemnity Benefit (AIB), Family Income Benefit (FIB) and Term Takaful Rider — as the rate book notes, the contribution rates of supplementary Takaful contracts are the same as those of conventional business
  • Unit allocations: 1st year 40% · 2nd year 80% · 3rd year 90% · 4th year onwards 100%
  • Surplus sharing: any surplus is shared 97.5% to participants as bonuses / 2.5% to the Operator’s Shareholders Fund (OSF), as printed in the rate book
  • Partial withdrawal: available after two membership years, subject to the conditions in the membership documents

An example (computed from the rate book’s table): at entry age 30 with a contribution paying term of 20 years, the table rate is Rs 47.53 per Rs 1,000 of Sum Cover — so for a Sum Cover of Rs 5,00,000, the annual contribution is 47.53 × 500 = Rs 23,765.

Please note: declared bonuses are announced under the Takaful model and the investment fund value depends on investment performance, which is borne by the participant — actual values may be lower or higher than any illustration. No fixed or guaranteed amount or return is promised.

Takaful Child Education & Marriage is a unit-linked, Shariah-compliant plan for building a lump sum for your child’s education and marriage.   • Participant entry age: 18 to 57 years

  • Minimum contribution: Annual Rs 15,000 · Semi-Annual Rs 10,000 · Quarterly Rs 5,000 · Monthly Rs 2,500
